ILLUSTRATIVE FMEA V1 — TESTING ONLY; NOT AN APPROVED CLIENT SOP
Score = severity * probability * detectability.
Each rating is an integer from 1 through 5. Higher detectability means harder to detect a failure.
Low = 1–19; Medium = 20–49; High = 50–125.
This example establishes calculation rules only. It does not establish clinical or regulatory acceptance criteria.
Severity, probability, and detectability ratings require documented justification and expert review.
Each risk must map to a requirement, identify a failure mode and impact, and identify a concrete mitigation.
